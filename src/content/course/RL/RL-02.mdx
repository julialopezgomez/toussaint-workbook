## Why this matters

RL-01 gave you the Bellman optimality equation, a single equation $V^*$ must satisfy. This module turns that equation into an actual algorithm, assuming (for now) full knowledge of the MDP: $P(s'\mid s,a)$ and $R(s,a)$ known exactly. **Value iteration** and **Q-iteration** are the model-based backbone of RL, and their convergence proof (a clean, self-contained argument) is the template every later convergence result in this block reuses.

## Value iteration

$V^*$ is a **fixed point** of the Bellman optimality equation: plugging $V^*$ into the right-hand side reproduces $V^*$ itself. This directly suggests an algorithm, **value iteration**: initialize $V_0(s)=0$ for all $s$, then repeatedly apply the Bellman equation as an *update rule* rather than a fixed-point condition:

$$V_{k+1}(s) = \max_a\Big[R(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)\,V_k(s')\Big], \qquad \forall s$$

iterating until $\max_s|V_{k+1}(s)-V_k(s)|\leq\epsilon$.

## Q-iteration

The identical idea applied to $Q^*(s,a)=R(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)\max_{a'}Q^*(s',a')$ (the Bellman optimality equation, in $Q$-form; note **the optimal policy reads off directly**, $\pi^*(s)=\operatorname*{argmax}_aQ^*(s,a)$, no separate maximization step needed once $Q^*$ is known):

$$Q_{k+1}(s,a) = R(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)\max_{a'}Q_k(s',a'), \qquad \forall s,a$$

## Convergence proof

<ExerciseCard id="RL-02-ex3-convergence-proof-derivation" />

**Theorem**: Q-iteration converges to $Q^*$. **Proof**: let $\Delta_k:=\|Q^*-Q_k\|_\infty=\max_{s,a}|Q^*(s,a)-Q_k(s,a)|$ (the worst-case error over every state-action pair). Then

$$Q_{k+1}(s,a) = R(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)\max_{a'}Q_k(s',a') \leq R(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)\max_{a'}\big[Q^*(s',a')+\Delta_k\big]$$

(using $Q_k(s',a')\leq Q^*(s',a')+\Delta_k$ for every $s',a'$, by definition of $\Delta_k$). Since $\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)=1$, the $\Delta_k$ term factors straight out:

$$= \Big[R(s,a)+\gamma\sum_{s'}P(s'\mid s,a)\max_{a'}Q^*(s',a')\Big]+\gamma\Delta_k = Q^*(s,a)+\gamma\Delta_k$$

(the bracketed term is exactly $Q^*(s,a)$'s own Bellman equation). By an identical argument in the other direction, $Q_{k+1}(s,a)\geq Q^*(s,a)-\gamma\Delta_k$. So $\Delta_{k+1}\leq\gamma\Delta_k$: **the error shrinks by a factor of $\gamma<1$ every iteration**, hence $\Delta_k\to0$ geometrically, and $Q_k\to Q^*$. The identical argument applies to value iteration.

## Summary

- **Value iteration** and **Q-iteration** turn the Bellman optimality equation from RL-01 into an iterative update rule, starting from $V_0=0$ (or $Q_0=0$) and converging to $V^*$ (or $Q^*$).
- $V^*(s)=\max_aQ^*(s,a)$ converts between them; $Q^*$ additionally hands you the optimal policy directly, $\pi^*(s)=\operatorname*{argmax}_aQ^*(s,a)$, with no extra step.
- **Convergence proof**: the worst-case error $\Delta_k=\|Q^*-Q_k\|_\infty$ shrinks by a factor of $\gamma$ every iteration, $\Delta_{k+1}\leq\gamma\Delta_k$, a **contraction mapping** argument, guaranteeing geometric convergence to $Q^*$.
